Why this exists

01

Said by us, not about us

Every word here is language neurodivergent people already use for themselves — never a clinical label, never someone else's diagnosis printed on your chest.

02

The word is the artwork

Each piece is drawn out of its own word — the letters scatter, spiral, sink, or trail off unfinished depending on what the feeling actually does to you.

03

Built to be easy to use

High contrast, generous spacing, and a low-stimulation mode that flattens the motion and turns the colour down — the toggle's in the header, use it freely.

The collection

Fifty pieces

Tees, hoodies, totes, notebooks, prints and mugs. Every piece is drawn out of its own word — and carries the Frog Logic frog, small, top right.

The frog in fancy dress: under a ghost sheet with only the eyes out, sprouting bat wings, climbing out of a grave marked Social Battery, and brewing quiet under a witch hat. The frogs

Comfort & Chaos · seven pieces

One small green creature having a very relatable time: wrapped in a blanket, running on 3%, holding a chilli about it. Each design is the Frog Logic frog — the one in the corner of everything else — finally getting the whole card to itself. Tees, mugs, a bottle and a print.

Digital

Thirty-three downloads + one made to order

No shipping, no waiting. Low-pressure planners and check-in tools, deliberately calmer than the prints — these are meant to be used on a hard day.

Made to order · built for one brain

The Bespoke Planner

Every planner you’ve ever bought was built for someone else’s brain. This one is assembled for yours: fifteen questions about how your day actually runs, how you track energy, what you keep forgetting, what a hard day looks like and who needs to understand your brain — then a planner of up to eighteen pages is built from that and emailed as a printable PDF, usually within a couple of hours.

DAILY PAGE IN YOUR SHAPE · YOUR HARD-DAY PAGE · A PAGE FOR WHOEVER NEEDS TO GET YOU · ROUTINES AS TICK-LISTS · EASY-READ OPTION

Answer the fifteen questions — £25

Undated, so print it as often as you like. If it doesn’t fit, answer again and we’ll rebuild it — free.

Not sure yet? Try one page first, free — three questions, your daily page, your name on it, straight away.

The Pond Guides

Twenty-three explainers · all free

One warm, plain-English guide per neurotype — what it actually feels like from the inside, what people get wrong, what genuinely helps, and what to say. Written to be handed to a relative, a partner, or a manager. The second set covers contested ground — conditions many people who have them call neurodivergent, and others don’t. Each of those says so on the first page.

These are free. No payment, no email address, no sign-up — the whole point of a guide like this is that it gets passed on, and a paywall sits exactly where you least want one. Take what you need and send it to whoever needs it.

For professionals

One set, more to come

For the people who meet a lot of different brains at work — teachers, GPs, managers, support workers, SENCOs. The individual guides above stay free for the people they’re about; this is the whole shelf in one file, for when you need all of it rather than one of it.

For organisations

One pack, three licences

Every sheet in the shop is sold for one person and their household. If you’re a SENCO, an OT service, a college, a GP practice or an employer’s ND network, you want to hand the same sheets to everyone you support — and a personal licence doesn’t let you. The Organisation Pack does: seventeen printable tools and all twenty-three Pond Guides in one 79-page file, with a plain-English licence to print them for every person you work with. Cards to hand over instead of explaining, sheets a person fills in about themselves and gives to you, pages for getting through the meeting, for the day to day, and for the weeks after an assessment.

Pick the licence that matches your size — nobody checks badges, and you can move up later by paying the difference. It never expires, new sheets come free for a year, and it downloads the moment you pay. Need an invoice first, or a purchase order? Email for one and it will be sorted the same day.
