Bespoke Planner Gift Card

Give someone a planner built for their brain. You get a printable gift card straight away to write in and hand over. They answer the fifteen questions at froglogic.co.uk/gift, type your email address as the gift code, and their own Bespoke Planner is built from their answers and emailed to them. Undated, up to eighteen pages, rebuilt free if it doesn't fit.

How it works: you pay, and the gift card arrives as a PDF straight away (A4 and US Letter). Print it, fold it, and write your email address in the gift code box, the one you paid with. That's what unlocks their planner, so one card is one planner.

They go to froglogic.co.uk/gift, answer fifteen questions about how their day runs, and type the code. Their planner is built from their answers and emailed to them, usually within a couple of hours. If it doesn't fit, they can answer again and it gets rebuilt free.

It's a good present for someone who's tried every planner and given up, or who's just been diagnosed and is working out how their brain runs. Not sure they'd like it? They can try one page free first.
