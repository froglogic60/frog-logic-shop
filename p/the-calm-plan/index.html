The Calm Plan

A gentle UK guide to getting ready for power cuts, floods and other emergencies, for neurodivergent adults and families. Ten free minutes that matter most, the official three-day checklist without the waste, a calm kit, first-hour scripts, an About me card and fill-in plan pages. A4 and US Letter.

The government now asks every UK household to be able to manage for three days if something goes wrong. For busy brains, a power cut or a flood warning isn't just an inconvenience: it's sudden change, noise, darkness and too many decisions at once. The Calm Plan is the gentle version: small steps, made calmly on an ordinary day, so on the hard day you just follow the plan you already wrote.

Inside: the ten free minutes that make the biggest difference (the Priority Services Register, flood warnings, numbers on paper, what the emergency alert siren means), the official three-day checklist with honest answers to cost, waste and space, a calm kit for noise, light, comfort and safe foods, step-by-step first-hour scripts, an About me card for helpers when words are hard, and fill-in pages that become your household's own plan. All the safety information comes from official UK sources, listed in the book.

You get two PDFs straight away, A4 and US Letter. Print the fill-in pages as often as you like for your own household. A paperback is on its way to Amazon too.
