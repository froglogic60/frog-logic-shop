Thank You Cards for SEN Staff

Three printable thank-you cards for the teaching assistant, teacher or SEN worker who gets your child: thank you for understanding me, thank you for being my safe person, and thank you for seeing me. The Comfort Creature frog on the front, a space for names inside. A4 and US Letter, fold in half.

You get two PDFs the moment you pay, A4 and US Letter. Each card is one landscape sheet folded in half: the frog on the front, the Frog Logic mark on the back, and a faint To and From inside. Personal use: print as many as you like for your own family; please don't resell or share the files.
