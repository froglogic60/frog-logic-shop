Free "how to help me" card maker

I'm Sam, I'm neurodivergent, and I run Frog Logic from my lounge. When it all gets too much, explaining what you need is the first thing to go. So make the card now, while it's easy, and hand it over later instead of finding the words. It's credit-card size, for a wallet, a lanyard or the back of a phone case.

The How To Help Me sheet (£2, printable) is the long one to hand over in advance: a please-don't-say / try-instead table, what helps day to day, exactly what to do if you shut down, and a space for the thing you most want people to know. The Access Needs Cards are six ready-made cards for appointments, shops and work.
