# Frog Logic — four weekly posts (final, Sam's versions)

## Week 1 — the free guides (give, don't sell)

Hi, I'm Sam, I'm neurodivergent, and I run a very small online shop called Frog Logic from my lounge.

The thing I'm proudest of isn't for sale. I wrote twenty-three short guides — autism, ADHD, AuDHD, dyslexia, dyspraxia, PDA, OCD and the rest — in plain English, for the person it's about AND for the people around them. The partner. The manager. The mum who still says "but you don't look autistic."

They're free. No email address, no sign-up, nothing to tick. Download the one you need and send it to whoever needs to read it. That's the whole idea.

froglogic.co.uk/#guides

(If one of them gets something wrong, tell me. I'd rather fix it than be right.)

## Week 2 — the planner, told as a story

Every planner I've ever bought was built for someone else's brain.

Hourly grids I never filled in. Habit trackers that turned into a record of failure by February. "Just write it down" — yes, but where, and in what order, and why does the page assume my day starts at 6am?

So I built one that asks first. Fifteen questions about how your day actually runs, what you keep forgetting, what a hard day looks like for you and what actually helps. Then a planner is put together from your answers and emailed to you as a PDF, usually within a couple of hours. Your own words are on the pages. There's a hard-day page you can tear in half and hand to someone.

It's £25, it's undated so you print it forever, and if it doesn't fit you answer again and it gets rebuilt free.

froglogic.co.uk/p/the-bespoke-planner/

with love
Sam

## Week 3 — the honest post

Small-business honesty post, because I think people like to know.

Frog Logic has only been open a very short while. Every bit of it — the shop, the guides, the planner that builds itself from your answers — was made by one neurodivergent person with a PC and a lot of help from an AI that does the coding and gets overruled regularly.

So far it has sold almost nothing. That's normal this early, apparently, and I'm not going anywhere. But if you've ever thought "I wish someone made a ___ for brains like mine", I would really like to hear it. The best things in the shop so far came from exactly that sentence. Comment or message me. No idea is too small or too specific.

I made it because I needed it. If you do too: froglogic.co.uk/p/when-words-go/ — £3, print it at home, laminate it if you can.
