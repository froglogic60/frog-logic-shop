# The approach list — ten organisations, one short email each

Written 10 Oct 2026 for the 90-day plan (step: 24 Oct, sent by Sam 27 Oct — done two weeks early).

## How to use this

I've written these as "I'm neurodivergent" rather than naming a diagnosis — swap in whatever you actually say about yourself; it reads better in your words than mine.

Send from hello@froglogic.co.uk, one email each, as plain text (no images, no PDF attached — the links do that job). Subject lines are given. Paste, read it once in your own head, change anything that doesn't sound like you, send. Where there is no public email, the org's contact form takes the same text.

Reply rate for cold email to charities is low — one or two replies out of ten is normal, not failure. The nudge on 8 Dec is already on the Desk.

## Why these ten, and not ten schools

The plan said SEN departments, OT services and employer ND networks. A single school's SENCO or one NHS OT team is a long shot cold — no local hook, one busy inbox, and they can't buy without a purchase order anyway. The organisations below each sit in front of hundreds or thousands of the people the sheets are for: SENCOs (nasen), parents and teachers (ADHD Embrace, Autistic Girls Network), adults in peer groups (ADHDadultUK, ADHD Aware), and employers (Genius Within, ADHD Foundation's workplace arm). One yes from any of them is worth fifty school emails. The free Pond Guides are the door-opener; the Organisation Pack is what some of them will buy once they've seen the quality.

Every contact route below was checked on 10 Oct 2026 against the Charity Commission register or the organisation's own site.
